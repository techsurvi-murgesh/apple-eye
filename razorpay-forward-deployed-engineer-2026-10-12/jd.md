## About Agent Studio

Agent Studio helps Razorpay merchants run AI agents across payments, checkout, disputes, subscriptions, reconciliation, risk, and customer workflows. They take the messy, undocumented work merchants do every day and turn it into reliable production agents.

## The Role

A Forward-Deployed Engineer who is equal parts engineer, solution consultant, and merchant partner — the single technical owner of the merchant relationship. Sitting directly with strategic merchants, running discovery, finding the real constraint behind the ask, designing the solution, building the first working version, proving whether it moved the metric, and expanding from there.

A deeply customer-facing role. You own the conversation — not hand it off. You walk into a room where the business owner, the ops lead, and the dashboard all tell different stories, and you're the one who reconciles them into a spec everyone agrees to ship. Then you go build it. You earn the customer's trust because you can do both — reshape the ask and write the code.

## What You'll Own

### Customer discovery & the relationship
- Own merchant conversations end-to-end — discovery calls, workshops, solution reviews, and rollout check-ins. You're the merchant's trusted technical advisor.
- Sit inside merchant workflows long enough to understand the real process, not the documented one. Ask who actually does the work and what happens when it fails.
- Reconcile conflicting signals across business owners, operators, dashboards, CRM, and ticket queues into a clear problem statement.

### Solution design & consulting
- Name the business constraint behind the ask: checkout recovery, dispute win rate, RTO loss, SIP recovery, settlement delay, refund spikes, support load, or cashflow leakage.
- Convert fuzzy merchant problems into crisp agent specs: success metric, data sources, tool calls, guardrails, human-in-the-loop points, and rollout plan.
- Decide what the agent does on its own, what it recommends, and where a human must approve.
- Write the spec clearly enough that product, engineering, legal, and the merchant all agree on what ships.

### Hands-on build & integration
- Build prototypes, connectors, MCP tool specs, eval fixtures, dashboards, and the glue code that gets an agent into production.
- Debug integration failures across Razorpay systems, merchant systems, external tools, and model behavior.
- Deploy across the constraints real merchants have — auth, networking, data access, latency, cost per run, observability, and compliance — and keep them in the design from day one.

### Proof, expansion & productization
- Set quality gates and outcome metrics before rollout. Track production outcomes, not demo applause.
- Measure whether the agent moved the target metric. If it didn't, diagnose and fix — or kill it with evidence.
- Drive expansion through building: spot the next high-value use case, prototype it, deepen the merchant's agent footprint.
- Turn repeated field patterns into reusable Agent Studio capabilities — connectors, templates, eval packs, docs, platform requirements.

## What This Role Is Not

- Not a pre-sales demo role where success is a slick walkthrough.
- Not a pure implementation role where success is shipping whatever the merchant asked for.

- Not a research role where success is a clever agent that never reaches production.
