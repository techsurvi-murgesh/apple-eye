# Senior Software Engineer, Cloud Software Development

## Role Overview

As a Senior Software Engineer in the Cloud Software Engineering team, you will design, develop, and operate production-grade distributed systems that power vehicle, charging, and mobile platforms. You will own complex services end-to-end and lead projects from technical design through implementation, deployment, and production operations, while working closely with cross-functional partners and contributing to the technical direction of the team.

## Key Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and operate scalable cloud services and distributed systems.
- Own services through design, development, deployment, and production support.
- Lead technical design discussions and code reviews.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Product, QA, and Mobile.
- Troubleshoot and resolve production issues in distributed environments.
- Leverage AI effectively across the SDLC to accelerate development, improve quality, and reduce toil.

## Required Qualifications

- 5+ years of professional software development experience.
- Strong proficiency in Golang.
- Production experience with cloud-native platforms and Kubernetes.
- Experience with NoSQL databases and event-driven architectures.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Strongly desirable: Proficiency in AI-Native Software Engineering, including effective use of AI tools and agents across requirements, design, coding, testing, debugging, code review, documentation, and operational workflows.
- Experience designing APIs for high-availability systems.
- Knowledge of Kafka, MQTT, or similar messaging technologies.
- Frontend development experience (for example, React, TypeScript, or modern web application development) is a plus.
- Experience mentoring junior engineers.
