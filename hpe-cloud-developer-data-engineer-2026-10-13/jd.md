## Job Description

Passionate about Cloud, Big Data, and AI? Join our team to build scalable data platforms and cloud-native solutions that power analytics, data science, and next-generation AI applications.

## What You'll Do

- Design, build, and maintain scalable data pipelines and cloud solutions.
- Develop automated data ingestion, processing, and optimisation workflows.
- Ensure data quality, reliability, and performance.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to solve complex data challenges.
- Evaluate and implement new technologies to enhance platform capabilities.

## What We're Looking For

- 2–4 years of experience in Data Engineering, Cloud Development, or a related field.
- Strong hands-on experience with Kafka, Spark, ClickHouse, ArangoDB, or similar technologies.
- Experience with AWS, Azure, or GCP.
- Proficiency in Python, Java, or Scala.
- Knowledge of Linux, Shell Scripting, CI/CD, and distributed systems.
- Exposure to Kubernetes, Docker, NoSQL databases, Message Queues, Elasticsearch, and visualisation tools.
- Understanding of Generative AI and Agentic AI concepts is a plus.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or a related discipline.
