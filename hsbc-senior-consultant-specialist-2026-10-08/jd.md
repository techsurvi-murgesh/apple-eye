# Senior Consultant Specialist — HSBC (HSBC Software Development India)

## Role

We are currently seeking an experienced professional to join our team in the role of Senior Consultant Specialist.

## In this role, you will

- Set up applications and onboard supporting infrastructure across development, UAT, pre-production and production environments.
- Design, implement and maintain CI/CD pipelines for reliable and repeatable deployments. Create consolidated, single-click production deployment pipelines using tools such as Jenkins and UrbanCode Deploy.
- Configure multi-stage deployment workflows and automate switching between production and pre-production servers.
- Manage application releases, deployment documentation, defect resolution and operational handovers.
- Monitor applications and infrastructure using tools such as ITRS Geneos, Splunk and Kibana.
- Perform root-cause analysis and troubleshoot application, build, configuration and environment-related issues.
- Set up, configure and monitor certificates and related application dependencies.
- Automate recurring testing and operational failures to reduce manual effort.
- Configure and manage batch jobs and scripts using Autosys and shell scripting.
- Deploy and manage cloud-native solutions using AWS, Docker and Kubernetes.
- Automate infrastructure and application deployment using Ansible, Helm and related DevOps tools.
- Manage source control and artefact repositories, including Git, Gerrit, Bitbucket and Nexus Artifactory.
- Support testing teams by diagnosing environment and deployment failures.
- Monitor and manage multiple highly available environments and critical production infrastructure.
- Develop scripts and automated reports for infrastructure analysis and operational reporting.
- Create and maintain configuration management and software change management processes.
- Collaborate with engineering, testing, operations and business teams to support successful releases.
- Provide technical guidance, documentation and knowledge transfer to new team members.

## Requirements

- Experience in DevOps, configuration management, release management or production support.
- Strong experience designing and troubleshooting CI/CD pipelines.
- Hands-on experience with: Kubernetes, Docker, AWS, Jenkins, Ansible, Shell scripting, Linux administration, Git-based source control.
- Experience with deployment and release tools.
- Knowledge of artefact management tools such as Nexus Artifactory.
- Experience with monitoring and observability tools, including Splunk, Kibana, ITRS Geneos.
