## Role Overview And Core Responsibilities

Infrastructure & Operations:

- Participate in the design, implementation, and maintenance of our infrastructure, ensuring reliability, scalability, and security.
- Support, monitor, and enhance the live infrastructure and platform solutions, ensuring high availability and performance.
- Help plan and execute the integration of our current infrastructure into TransUnion's group-wide cloud platform while minimising disruptions.
- Participate in the migration of infrastructure from Legacy to Hybrid cloud.

DevOps & Security:

- Maintain robust CI/CD pipelines, collaborating closely with development teams to streamline deployment processes.
- Maintain and enhance our security posture, ensuring compliance with industry standards and frameworks (e.g., SOC-2, ISO 27001).
- Diagnose and resolve infrastructure outages and incidents, ensuring timely resolution and root cause analysis.
- Documentation & Best Practices:
- Ensure comprehensive documentation of infrastructure, systems, and processes to support onboarding, troubleshooting, and scalability.
- Promote and implement DevOps and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) best practices across the organization.

## Required Knowledge And Experiences

- At least 3+ years of Development Ops experience on Hybrid cloud experience, operating high availability systems on AWS
- Knowledge of SRE best practices, i.e. developing tools that enhance the system performance, reliability and developer experience
- 2+ years of hands on experience with Container Orchestration, performance tuning, Docker and Kubernetes in cloud production environment
- Cloud and automation engineering experience in a mission-critical environment
- Strong skills and experience through the CI/CD stack that deploys to infrastructure elements such as Kubernetes
- Troubleshooting using observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, Splunk, etc in complex cloud based environments.
- Experience with: IaC (ie. Terraform), Ansible, Docker, Kubernetes/EKS, Secrets Management, Gitops and all things AWS
- Experience writing code/scripts in a modern programming language like Python or Java
- Past experiences with Monitoring tools such as Splunk, Grafana would be desirable.
