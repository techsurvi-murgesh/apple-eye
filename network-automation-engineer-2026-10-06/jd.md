# Network Automation Engineer (Service Provider)

Minimum Qualifications:

Requires BS/BE/BTech degree or equivalent with 2-4 years of experience in a similar job role.
Any Service Provider related certification (CCNA/CCNP - DevOps) is helpful and you have a drive for continuous learning.
Understanding in handling Network Management (NMS) tools/applications
Good understanding/Experience with SQL databases and Postgres SQL
Knowledge of software programmability, automation (Python, YAML, JSON, GitHub, Ansible, Terraform and so on), orchestration, and/or virtualization technologies.
Advance level of Linux/Unix OS administration & troubleshooting
Understanding of Virtualisation technologies (VMware, Docker, Kubernetes);
Understanding of Agentic AI Tools and Industry Models.

Preferred Qualifications:

Understanding of relevant Cisco technologies and delivery methods and builds skills in articulating technical concepts.
Foundational consulting and collaboration skills
Knowledge and hands-on experience on NCS540, NCS560, ASR9k NCS55xx and Cisco 8k platforms.
