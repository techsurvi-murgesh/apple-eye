# Senior Software Engineer — Zscaler

Bangalore | Hybrid | Reporting to Director of Software Engineering

## About Zscaler

At Zscaler, we are building security products that enable organizations to adopt AI applications and agents safely and at scale. We foster an inclusive environment that values all backgrounds and perspectives, emphasizing collaboration and belonging. Our mission is to make doing business seamless and secure.

## Role Overview

We are building security products that enable organizations to adopt AI applications and agents safely and at scale. This is a hybrid capacity based in Bangalore role, reporting to the Director of Software Engineering. You will act as a hands-on Senior Software Engineer leading key product capabilities, taking full ownership of complex technical initiatives, shaping architectural direction, and guiding other engineers.

## What You'll Do (Role Expectations)

- Lead the design and delivery of major product capabilities, owning projects from problem definition through implementation, rollout, and production operation.
- Design secure, reliable, and scalable systems with clearly defined trust boundaries, making technical decisions that balance security, privacy, performance, reliability, and usability.
- Lead complex investigations while improving engineering practices, testing, and operational readiness.
- Mentor engineers and provide direction through design and code reviews.
- Partner with product and research teams while contributing to technical strategy and longer-term architecture.

## Who You Are (Success Profile)

- Thrive in ambiguity; comfortable building the path as you walk it in a dynamic environment.
- Act like an owner; passion for the mission fuels a bias for action; operate with integrity.
- Problem-solver; laser-focused on finding solutions to hard problems.
- High-trust collaborator; ambitious for the team; embrace challenge culture with candid feedback.
- Learner with a growth mindset, actively seeking feedback to become a stronger teammate.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Foundational understanding of AI/ML technologies and experience leveraging, securing, or positioning AI-driven solutions to optimize outcomes.
- 3-4 years of software-engineering experience, including ownership of complex production systems and a demonstrated ability to lead technically ambiguous, cross-functional projects.
- Deep expertise in systems engineering, distributed systems, security engineering, backend platforms, or endpoint software, combined with strong proficiency in Go, Rust, and Python.
- Experience designing systems with clear security, privacy, and reliability requirements, alongside a strong understanding of concurrency, networking, storage, APIs, and operational observability.
- Experience in managing Cloud Infrastructure on one of the Hyperscalers: AWS, GCP, or Azure.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Proven ability to leverage AI technologies and workflows to drive measurable operational efficiency.
- Experience building security, cloud, or enterprise software, including endpoint software for Windows, MacOS, and Linux.
- Familiarity with AI applications, agents, developer platforms, or AI security.
- Experience with security policy, detection, analysis, or software supply-chain systems.
