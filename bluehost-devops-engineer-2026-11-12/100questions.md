# 100 Interview Questions — DevOps Engineer (Bluehost)

## Role & Responsibilities

1. What does it mean for DevOps to make sure every other team can ship fast without breaking things?
2. How do you own pipelines, cloud-native infrastructure, and observability at scale (millions to tens of millions of customers)?
3. How do you modernize legacy pieces while keeping the platform fast, safe, and reliable?
4. How do you use AI tooling to move faster than a team of your size normally could?
5. How do you design, build, and operate Kubernetes and cloud-native infrastructure across multiple clouds?
6. How do you cover compute, storage, networking, and IAM across AWS, GCP, Azure, and/or OCI?
7. How do you own Infrastructure as Code using Terraform version-controlled in Git across environments?
8. How do you align IaC with team-wide best practices?
9. How do you build and maintain CI/CD pipelines with Jenkins and Argo (CD/Workflows)?
10. How do you automate build, test, and release for dozens of services?

11. How do you drive release and deployment management practices?
12. How do you design progressive rollouts and rollback strategies?
13. How do you implement change management that reduces risk without slowing teams down?
14. How do you configure and harden web servers (Apache/Nginx), virtual hosts, and SSL/TLS across environments?
15. How do you build out observability, metrics, tracing, and log aggregation (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK/Loki)?
16. How do you create real alerting that catches issues before customers do?
17. How do you use AI across the delivery lifecycle — scripting, infrastructure code, root-cause investigation, and runbooks?
18. How do you set guardrails for AI-generated infrastructure code before it touches production?
19. How do you check provenance and licensing for AI-generated code?
20. How do you set boundaries for what customer or secret data enters a prompt?

21. How do you own networking fundamentals in production: TCP/IP, DNS, firewalls (iptables/nftables), load balancing?
22. How do you handle networking across cloud and on-prem environments?
23. How do you carry on-call rotation and lead incident response?
24. How do you turn postmortems into concrete reliability improvements?
25. How do you modernize legacy infrastructure safely?
