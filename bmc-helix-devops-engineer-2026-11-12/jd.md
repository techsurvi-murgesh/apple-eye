Our BMC Helix portfolio delivers modern, cloud-native SaaS solutions for IT Service Management (ITSM), IT Operations Management (ITOM), and AIOps. Within IT Operations Management (ITOM), BMC Helix empowers organizations to proactively monitor, diagnose, and remediate issues across complex hybrid and multi-cloud environments. Now, we're looking for a DevOps Engineer to join our team and play a strong role in shaping the future of ITOM delivery.

## Here is how, through this exciting role, YOU will contribute to BMC's and your own success

- Contribute to monthly deployments and upgrades of the ITOM SaaS suite across 20+ global data centers
- Contribute to semi-annual upgrades of services like Victoria Metrics, OpenSearch, Redis, and Kafka across AWS, Oracle, and Google Cloud.
- Migrate customers between ITOM instances, apply hotfixes, implement change requests, and resolve service requests.
- Remediate monitoring alerts, contribute to automation initiatives, and implement reliable processes that scale.

## Skillset & Experience

- 4+ years of experience in a DevOps role
- Bachelor's Degree in a related field; certifications such as CKA, AWS, GCP, OCI are a plus
- Strong experience with Kubernetes, including Helm charts
- Proficiency in Shell scripting (absolute must); additional scripting/automation experience with Python and Java is desirable
- Hands-on experience implementing CI/CD pipelines using tools such as Jenkins and Spinnaker, following best practices
- Knowledge of Terraform & Ansible is desirable
- Familiarity with version control systems (Git) and project management tools (JIRA)
- Experience with at least one or all of AWS, Google Cloud, and Oracle Cloud
- Exposure to AWS managed services such as Kafka, OpenSearch, ElastiCache, and Postgres/RDS is a plus
- Experience with monitoring tools such as Kibana, Fluentbit is a plus
