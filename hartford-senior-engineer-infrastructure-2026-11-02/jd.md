**Job Title:** Senior Engineer, Infrastructure (IND GCC034)
**Company:** The Hartford
**Position:** Insurance leader (property & casualty, employee benefits, mutual funds)
**Experience:** 3 - 5 years
**Education:** Bachelor's degree in computer science or related discipline

## About The Company

Our client is a leader in property and casualty insurance, employee benefits and mutual funds. One of the largest insurers in the United States with many decades of expertise, this company is widely recognized for its service excellence, sustainability practices, trust, and integrity.

## Position Overview

We are seeking a Staff Infrastructure Engineer with deep expertise in designing, implementing, and managing cloud-native database solutions running on Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Google Cloud. The solutions include relational and No-SQL products.

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and implement cloud-based database architectures that meet application requirements and organizational goals.
- Collaborate with application teams to architect scalable, secure, and high-performing cloud database solutions that align with enterprise standards.
- Partner with the Cloud Engineering and Site Reliability Engineers to integrate database CI/CD pipelines into their application deployments. Including Jenkins and Ansible.
- Have in-depth knowledge of designing and building automation for database provisioning and repetitive tasks using infrastructure as code tools, including AWS's CloudFormation and Terraform.
- Develop and maintain disaster recovery and high availability strategies. Provide DR testing support.
- Ensure compliance with data governance, security, regulatory and privacy standards. Capability with preparing evidence to support internal and external auditors.
- Proficient at preparing deployment and operational procedure manuals.
- Provide second-level support for troubleshooting production incidents.
- Design and implement native cloud database solutions using Terraform and AWS's CloudFormation.
- Automate the monitoring and alerting of database performance and availability.
- Provide support for the operational support team on routine database maintenance, including backups, patching, performance tuning, and capacity planning.
- Master the latest DB features and apply them to provide measurable value to application teams.
- Perform analysis and end-to-end execution from roadmap to implementation of database consolidations, migrations, and conversions.
- Work to reduce complexity and cost in database environments through analysis, simplification, and automation using Ansible, Python, Terraform or other tools.
- Evaluate new product releases and make recommendations on implementation of newest versions.
- Assist application and DBA teams with databases migration from an on-prem system to the Cloud.
- Maintain documentation of standard operating policies and procedures.
