# Cloud Platform & DevOps Engineer — Asset Vantage

**Company:** Asset Vantage
**Location:** Pune
**Experience:** 2 to 6 years
**Posted:** 2026-11-02

## Key Responsibilities

- Lead the design, implementation, and operation of secure, scalable, and reliable cloud platforms.
- Own and improve CI/CD pipelines, release automation, infrastructure-as-code, and deployment standards.
- Design and manage AWS identity and access solutions, including Amazon Cognito, IAM, authentication, authorization, and access-control patterns.
- Establish DevSecOps practices across development, testing, deployment, and production operations.
- Coordinate and remediate Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) findings.
- Ensure infrastructure and deployment practices meet applicable security, audit, and compliance requirements.
- Lead incident response, root-cause analysis, corrective actions, and operational improvements.
- Provide technical solutions for complex infrastructure, application deployment, security, and performance issues.
- Partner with Engineering, Security, Compliance, QA, and Product teams on architecture and release decisions.
- Define monitoring, logging, alerting, backup, disaster recovery, and environment-management standards.
- Support cloud-cost governance and optimization in collaboration with FinOps stakeholders.
- Mentor junior DevOps engineers through technical guidance, code reviews, troubleshooting sessions, and structured learning plans.
- Create and maintain architecture documents, runbooks, SOPs, deployment procedures, and control evidence.

## Required Technical Skills

- Strong AWS experience, including EC2, RDS, VPC, IAM, Security Groups, CloudWatch, ECS/Fargate, EKS/Kubernetes, S3, Route 53, and Lambda.
- Hands-on experience with Amazon Cognito, including user pools, identity pools, federation, MFA, OAuth/OIDC, JWT tokens, and role-based access control.
- Strong CI/CD experience with Jenkins, Bitbucket Pipelines, Git, and automated deployment workflows.
- Experience with Infrastructure as Code using AWS CDK, Terraform, CloudFormation, or similar tools.
- Strong Docker and Kubernetes experience, including container security and deployment troubleshooting.
- Working knowledge of Linux, networking, scripting, and automation using Python, Bash, or PowerShell.
- Experience with monitoring and logging platforms such as CloudWatch, Grafana, Datadog, ELK, or Splunk.
- Experience supporting VAPT activities, assessing findings, prioritizing remediation, and tracking closure.
- Understanding of security controls, vulnerability management, encryption, secrets management, access reviews, and secure configuration baselines.
- Knowledge of compliance requirements and audit evidence practices, such as SOC2 internal controls.

## Leadership Requirements

- Proven ability to lead a DevOps or platform engineering team.
- Ability to break down complex technical problems into practical, risk-based solutions.
- Strong mentoring skills with a focus on developing junior engineers.
- Ability to set engineering standards, review technical designs, and improve delivery practices.
- Clear communication with both technical teams and business, audit, and compliance stakeholders.
- Ownership mindset, sound judgment during incidents, and a continuous-improvement approach.

## Qualification

- BE / B.Tech.
