## Position Summary

AI & Engineering leverages cutting-edge engineering capabilities to help build, deploy, and operate integrated/verticalized sector solutions in software, data, AI, network, and hybrid cloud infrastructure. These insights are powered by engineering for business advantage, helping transform mission-critical operations.

Join our AI & Engineering team to help transform technology platforms, driving innovation, and help make a significant impact on our clients' achievements. You’ll work alongside talented professionals reimagining and re-engineering operations and processes that could be critical to businesses.

Deloitte is seeking a DevOps Engineer II to support the design, build, deployment, and optimization of cloud-based engineering solutions. In this role, you will contribute across the software development lifecycle, help strengthen DevOps and automation capabilities, and work with cross-functional teams to deliver scalable, reliable outcomes for clients. The role offers an opportunity to apply engineering skills in cloud infrastructure, continuous integration and delivery, observability, and platform modernization.

## Work you'll do

As a DevOps Engineer II on the Engineering as a Service team, you will be responsible for:

- Gather and analyze requirements with consideration for business and sector needs.
- Design, build, deploy, and support DevOps pipelines and cloud-based engineering solutions across the software development lifecycle.
- Configure and manage infrastructure as code, automation, and platform services in Amazon Web Services environments.
- Participate in Agile delivery activities, including sprint planning, retrospectives, estimation, and peer reviews.
- Contribute to knowledge sharing, process improvement, and innovation efforts that improve delivery quality and efficiency.

## Qualifications

### Required

- 3-6 years of experience developing continuous integration and continuous delivery pipelines using Amazon Web Services DevOps services, including CodeBuild, CodeDeploy, and CodePipeline
- Experience with at least one continuous integration and continuous delivery orchestration tool, including Jenkins, GitHub Actions, or GitLab CI/CD
- Experience building and provisioning Amazon Web Services infrastructure using infrastructure as code, including Terraform, Amazon Web Services CloudFormation, or Ansible
- Experience with Amazon Web Services services such as Lambda, Glue, database services, EventBridge, Elastic Kubernetes Service, Simple Storage Service, security groups, and API Gateway
- Experience deploying microservices in Kubernetes environments and creating orchestration files
- Experience with scripting using YAML and JSON
- Bachelor of Engineering, Bachelor of Technology, Master of Computer Applications, Master of Science in Computer Science, or equivalent degree

### Preferred

- Experience implementing observability solutions using Dynatrace, Datadog, AppDynamics, Elastic Stack, or Splunk
- Experience implementing site reliability engineering practices, including service-level indicators, service-level objectives, error budgets, auto-remediation, and auto-healing
- Experience with branching strategies aligned to software release approaches
- Experience managing monitoring tools for application and platform observability
