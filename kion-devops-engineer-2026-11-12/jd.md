## About Kion

Kion is a company that relies on a DevOps team to support day-to-day operations and deliver automation and platform improvement initiatives. This position will increase the DevOps team's capacity to support day-to-day operations and deliver automation and platform improvement initiatives. Locating the role in Pune, India, will broaden support coverage across time zones, enabling more timely assistance for global development teams and smoother handovers between regions. The role will strengthen both operational support capacity and the ability to deliver planned improvements.

## Role Overview

As a DevOps Engineer, you will take responsibility for the operation, automation, maintenance, and further development of internal DevOps tools and platforms. You will work closely with colleagues in the Application DevOps Platform and software development teams to deliver reliable and maintainable solutions.

## Responsibilities

- Administer, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve developer tools and supporting infrastructure.
- Develop and maintain CI/CD pipelines and support reliable build, test, and deployment workflows.
- Operate and troubleshoot Linux-based services, including configuration, access, networking, and TLS certificates.
- Investigate application logs, pipeline failures, and infrastructure issues; collaborate with developers to identify root causes.
- Automate recurring tasks and infrastructure configuration using scripts and suitable automation tools.
- Support containerized workloads and Kubernetes environments as required.
- Analyze interfaces and dependencies between tools and services to develop effective integrated solutions.
- Evaluate new tools and improvements, validate their suitability, and coordinate rollouts with other teams.
- Plan and execute changes with appropriate testing, recovery arrangements, and documentation.
- Balance operational support with project work, proactively identifying and delivering improvements.

## Tasks and Qualifications

The role involved Jenkins, GitHub, Jira, Confluence, SonarQube, JFrog Artifactory, Azure, Terraform, Ansible, Docker, Kubernetes, and Nginx. Java knowledge is beneficial for understanding application behavior and troubleshooting.

We value depth of practical experience in several relevant areas and the ability to transfer that knowledge to unfamiliar tools. Experience administering platforms, including configuration, permissions, upgrades, and troubleshooting, is particularly relevant.
